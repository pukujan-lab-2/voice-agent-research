# 0019. Market positioning: the assistant is never framed as a neighbour

**Status:** Accepted · **Date:** 2026-11-02 · **Issue:** #111

## Decision

How we present the assistant, in its name, page copy, ads, videos and pictures:

1. **No neighbour framing.** Never put the assistant together with neighbour, neighbourhood, next door, or a local or community watch.
2. **No Spider-Man nod.** No "friendly neighbourhood", no spider imagery.
3. **Asked, never pushed.** It shows up when someone asks for help. Copy never says it watches, follows, or checks in on its own.
4. **Not someone else's brand.** We don't use "Copilot", another company's product name. The assistant gets a human first name, chosen separately.

## Why

People's worry about AI right now is that it's pushed into everything, and that neighbourhood camera networks like Flock Safety track people across whole towns. A helper "next door" sounds like part of that, not the help someone chose to ask for. The owner caught this on 2026-11-02 while we were picking a name. The candidate was "Peter", as in "friendly neighbourhood".

## Consequences

- Name ideas and copy get checked against this list before they reach the owner.
- The home page rename (#109) picks a human name with no neighbour meaning.
- Stories keep the help as something the person chose: they ask, and it answers.
