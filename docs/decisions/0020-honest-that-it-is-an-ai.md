# 0020. The assistant is honest that it's an AI

**Status:** Accepted · **Date:** 2026-11-02 · **Issue:** #113

## Decision

We present the assistant as what it is: an AI helper on a website.

1. **No human name.** Its name says what it does. The word "AI" sits next to the name wherever the name appears, including the logo lockup.
2. **No face.** No avatar, no character drawing, no first-person backstory. The logo is a simple mark, not a person.
3. **It says so itself.** It talks as an AI and never claims feelings or a life of its own. If someone asks whether it's a person, it says no.
4. **People stay people.** On our pages, the humans are the people being helped. The assistant is the tool they chose to use. When someone needs a person, it brings one in.

## Why

When an AI is kept as an AI instead of being made to look human, people respond better. It's also honest, and it goes with [0019](0019-market-positioning-not-a-neighbour.md): people push back on AI that's shoved at them or pretends to be one of them. The owner decided this on 2026-11-02, after first leaning toward a human name.

## Consequences

- Name ideas that are human first names are out. The shortlist is in [the brand, marketing and UX guide](../brand-marketing-ux.md).
- Voice agent prompts keep the assistant speaking as an AI.
- Marketing pictures show the people being helped, never a face for the assistant.
