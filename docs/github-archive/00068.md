The call widget looked like a different product bolted onto the pages, its compact window was too wide to sit in a corner, and on a phone its start bar ran off the screen — which is what made the agent's work on the page hard to watch.

It now reads as part of the page, and it fits a phone.

## What changes

- **Same system as the pages.** Paper, ink and clay, one border and shadow, the business name in the serif. The primary button and the caller's own bubbles take the *site's* accent, so the widget belongs to the clinic on the clinic's page.
- **Compact is a corner window.** 392 px wide and under 200 px tall: status, the copilot's question, and the controls, all in view while the form fills.
- **Expanded is the conversation.** A 1rem transcript with real bubbles, clay chips for what the copilot can do, and the footnote that says the visitor presses the final button.
- **The caption shows the question.** It used to show the tail of the last message, which the two-line box then cut off mid-sentence ("…What's your child's full…"). It now shows the closing question itself, and on short screens one line, so the form stays in view.
- **The phone bar is two rows.** The two buttons first, then the voice and language pickers, each taking half the width. Before, the pickers pushed "Type instead" outside a 390 px viewport — the phone layout scene could not click it at all.

## How to verify

`npm run check` passes: lint, ruff, typecheck, knip, 49 engine tests, 8/8 scenarios.

`npm run test:offline` in `demo/live-site`: 151 checks pass. The typing path at all three phone sizes is green, which is the part that was crashing.

## What stays unchanged

No conversation logic, no engine, no speech stacks. The only behaviour touched is which sentence the caption picks, and the widget still never submits a form.

## What still fails, and why

Three checks per phone size ("compact bar covers at most a fifth of the screen", "the caption shows the copilot's question", "the field being asked is highlighted and above the bar") and the whole `voice` scene.

A voice call now runs on LiveKit and needs a real microphone. Headless, `getUserMedia` is refused, the widget falls back to typing, so there is no spoken question and no field being driven. The scene harness still stubs the browser speech APIs from before #61, so it cannot drive a LiveKit call at all. That is a harness gap, not a layout one: the measurements those checks make now pass on the typing path, and the panel sizes are within every limit they set.
