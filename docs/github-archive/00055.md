# Issue #55: Voice call only works in English, though visitors must be able to talk in their own language

- State: open
- Opened by Pukujan on 2026-11-01
- Original: https://github.com/Pukujan-lab/voice-agent-research/issues/55

### Summary

PROJECT.md promises visitors can talk to the agent "in their own language", but the voice call merged in PR #38 only works in English. Each of its models and settings is English-only: speech to text, the turn detector, and the voice output. That choice was never recorded as a decision or a known limit, so a visitor who speaks Spanish or Chinese on a call gets misheard or ignored, and nothing in the record warns anyone.

### Identity and Lineage

Leaf: this issue. Parent: none. Depends on: none. Related: #11 and PR #38 (the LiveKit voice call), decision 0002 (voice on LiveKit Agents), decision 0011 (confirmation policy; read-backs must work in the caller's language).

### Acceptance Criteria

- The owner picks the speech models for a multilingual call, and a decision records the pick.
- Speech to text, turn detection and voice output each handle the languages that decision names, and the call replies in the language the caller speaks.
- The reported language comes from the recogniser, not a fixed `'en'`.
- PROJECT.md's status table states which languages voice supports.

### Boundaries and Next Action

In scope: `demo/live-site/speech/agent.py`, `demo/live-site/speech/server.py`, the speech model setup, the decision records, PROJECT.md's status row. Out of scope: text chat (it already passes the visitor's words through), phone calls (#SIP planned). Next action: the owner picks the speech models.

### Symptoms

1. **Speech to text is English-only.** `speech/server.py` loads NVIDIA Parakeet TDT 0.6B **v2**, an English model, and `speech/agent.py` labels every transcript `language='en'`.
2. **Turn detection is English-only.** `speech/agent.py` uses `livekit.plugins.turn_detector.english.EnglishModel`, so it judges end of turn on English patterns.
3. **Voice output is English-only.** Pocket TTS speaks English; the Kokoro fallback is called with `lang='en-us'`.
4. **No record of the limit.** Decision 0002 and the PR #38 task record don't say the call is English-only; PROJECT.md lists multi-language as "Planned" without naming the gap in the shipped call.

### Hypotheses (unverified)

- **H1. The English-only stack was picked for speed on an M1, not as a product choice.** Status: unverified. Would confirm: `speech/server.py`'s own note that Parakeet beat faster-whisper small.en on latency. Would refute: a recorded decision limiting voice to English. Experiment: none; read the PR #38 history.

### Counter-signal

The latency notes in `speech/server.py` show the English models were chosen for a fast first reply, which a multilingual stack may cost.

### Honest Caveat

Language coverage differs by model. From their model cards: LFM3-Audio-2B covers 18 languages for speech in and out, but its model card says live speech recognition and voice chat run in English only. Parakeet v3 covers 25 European languages. Whisper large-v3-turbo covers about 99. `docs/08-speech-model-licenses.md` recommends Parakeet v3 with Whisper large-v3-turbo, plus Kokoro. LiveKit ships a `MultilingualModel` turn detector. None of this is decided here.

### Problems vs Gaps

Problem: the shipped voice call breaks the "in their own language" promise. Gap: no decision records which languages voice supports.

### Proposal *(proposal)*

The owner picks the speech stack; then swap the three English-only pieces for multilingual ones and record it. Nothing here exists yet.
