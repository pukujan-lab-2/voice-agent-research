**Problem.** The widget still talks through the browser's own speech, which goes against decision 0002 (#4). In the live test on 2026-10-31 it couldn't hear, the intro ran long, the voice sounded robotic, and "can you hear me" went to search. The LiveKit rebuild that fixes this was committed on 2026-10-31 but never pushed.

**Before and after**

| | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Listening | Browser `SpeechRecognition` | Parakeet TDT 0.6B (sherpa-onnx), hosted by us |
| Speaking | Browser `speechSynthesis` | Pocket TTS, hosted by us |
| Turn taking | None, so the caller can't interrupt | Silero VAD and LiveKit's turn detector; talking over the voice stops it |
| Intro | Long, spoken in full | One short line spoken, full greeting on screen |
| "Can you hear me" | Sent to search | Answered at once, never searched |

**What changes**
- `demo/live-site/speech/agent.py`: the LiveKit Agents worker. It listens, ends each turn and speaks. The copilot engine in the page still decides every reply, over LiveKit RPC (`copilot.greet`, `copilot.turn`, `agent.say`).
- `demo/live-site/speech/server.py`: Parakeet and Pocket TTS on this machine. `requirements.txt` pins `livekit-agents` 1.8.8 and says where the models come from. The models aren't in git.
- `demo/live-site/widget/sdk.ts`: the browser speech calls are gone. A call asks for the microphone first, then joins the LiveKit room.
- `demo/live-site/server.ts`: starts the parts of the voice stack it can. Each turn's timings go to `speech/turns.jsonl`.
- `demo/live-site/tests/voice-call.ts`: a new test that plays recorded audio into a fake microphone, so every turn goes through the real stack.

**How to verify.** Start `livekit-server --dev`, `speech/server.py` and `speech/agent.py`, then run `node --no-warnings tests/voice-call.ts` in `demo/live-site`. The commit reports 9 of 9 checks passing: small talk got a reply 0.75 to 0.8 s after the caller stopped speaking, the intro took 3.6 s, and barge-in stopped the voice in about 0.36 s. `tests/tax-call.ts` passed 106 of 106. Those figures come from the session that wrote the code; this push didn't rerun them. CI doesn't run the audio test, because it needs the speech stack.

**What stays the same.** The copilot engine's rules and the tax site's flows. Only how speech gets in and out changes.
