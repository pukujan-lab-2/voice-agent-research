# Issue #104: Find the right form on a large site by search instead of loading every flow

- State: open
- Opened by Pukujan on 2026-11-01
- Original: https://github.com/Pukujan-lab/voice-agent-research/issues/104

### Summary

A site with many pages and forms can't keep every flow in memory, and the widget matches "take me to pricing" against the full page list in the page. The page and form map should be indexed in Octo so the copilot finds the form for the current page or the visitor's request by search and loads only that one.

### Identity and Lineage

Leaf: this issue. Parent: #100. Depends on: per-business config (the third child). Related: Octo search over documents (already used for answers).

### Acceptance Criteria

- Each form of a business is indexed as an Octo document scoped to that business, and its flow stored by business and form id.
- A lookup by the current page path or by the visitor's words returns the right form, and only that flow is loaded.
- Test: a synthetic site of 200 forms; 30 queries find the right form, at most one flow is loaded per lookup, and another business's forms are never returned.

### Boundaries and Next Action

In scope: a site-index module, an endpoint, an indexing tool, tests. Out of scope: reading forms from live pages (page.ts already does that). Next action: write the index.
