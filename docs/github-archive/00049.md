On the tax demo, "can I ask you about taxes?" was searched in the IRS guides and got an unrelated passage.

## Problem and consequence

Anything not on the small-talk list goes to the guide search. Questions about the assistant itself ("can I ask questions?", "what can you help with?") opened IRS Publication 970 or got "I couldn't find that in the tax guides". This is usually the first thing a caller says.

## What changes

- A new "help" kind on the small-talk list in `widget/sdk.ts` covers: can I ask (you) (a question / questions) (about taxes), can you help me, what can you do, who are you, are you a bot, how does this work.
- On a desk site the answer is: "Yes, go ahead. I'm the Brindlewick Tax & Accounting assistant. I answer from the IRS guides and show you the part I'm quoting, and I can help you book a consultation. For advice on your own situation, a preparer can help. What's your question?" On a form site it offers to fill in the form or change page.
