# Issue #56: Confirm each field by its risk score on the live site

- State: closed (2026-11-01)
- Opened by Pukujan on 2026-11-01
- Original: https://github.com/Pukujan-lab/voice-agent-research/issues/56
- Labels: status:resolved

### Summary

The live site's desk reads back every value and waits for a yes, whatever the field. A time slot gets the same "Tuesday 9:40, is that right?" as a phone number. Decision 0011 (merged in #53) asks for something finer: each field and action gets a risk score from 1 to 100, and the score sets how the agent confirms. The #16 wiring does not do this yet, so this issue carries it.

### Identity and Lineage

Leaf: this issue. Parent: none. Depends on: #16 (the v2 desk on the live site). Related: #52 (the confirmation policy).

### Acceptance Criteria

- Each business config gives a risk score per field and action. The default bands live in one place that both the prompt and the code lock read.
- Scores 11–30 are mentioned in passing with no yes needed. Scores 31–55 are read back and wait for a yes, as every field does today.
- After the caller corrects a misheard value, each remaining step scores 15 higher.
- From 56 up, code refuses the action without a recorded yes. From 76 it asks once more, and from 91 a person approves.
- The confirmations asked for and received go to Phoenix, so evals can check that every action scoring 56 or more had its yes.
- A desk test covers each band and the +15 rule.

### Boundaries and Next Action

In scope: `copilot-engine/src/desk.ts`, the site's flow configs and the widget. Out of scope: the NYC map agent, which scores 30 or below everywhere. Next action: add the score to the field type and the default bands.

### Symptoms

1. **Every field is confirmed the same way.** Low-stakes choices get a full read-back and a wait for yes.
2. **No score exists in any config.** Nothing records how much a field or action matters.

### Hypotheses (unverified)

- **H1. Most demo fields map to 31–55, and only slots and times drop to 11–30.** Status: unverified. Would confirm: the site's five forms score with no field needing a new band. Would refute: a field that needs behaviour the bands cannot express. Experiment: score the five forms on paper first.

### Counter-signal

Reading back everything is safe today. The desk never presses submit, so nothing reaches 56 or above yet.

### Honest Caveat

The band limits come from decision 0011 and are a starting point; changing them needs a new decision.

### Problems vs Gaps

Problem: low-stakes fields cost the caller an extra turn. Gap: no risk score in the configs, no lock for 56 and up.

### Proposal *(proposal)*

Add `risk?: number` to the field type with per-kind defaults, then choose the confirmation step from the band. None of this exists yet.
