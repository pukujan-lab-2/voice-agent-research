# PR #125: Make the tax copilot ask what vague questions mean and stop dropping right answers

- State: open
- Opened by Pukujan on 2026-11-01
- Original: https://github.com/Pukujan-lab/voice-agent-research/pull/125
- Branch `task/WCP-0027-conversation-quality` into `main`, +14234 −32 in 22 files

The owner called the tax demo and it "still seemed too dumb": it quoted a payments help line for "How do I find my taxes?", answered "Yeah." with an offer nobody had made, and said "I don't know" to a question the library answers because Jev's own pick (0.70) lost to a cut-off on "none" (0.26 against 0.25).

## What changes

- **The judge rule.** A pick now stands when "none" is unlikely (0.25 or less, as before) or when Jev's own probability for the picked passage is at least 0.65 and at least 0.35 above "none". The judge span in Phoenix now carries Jev's probabilities, the rule that applied and any error (a failed Jev call was a silent "none").
- **Asking back.** `copilot-engine/src/dialogue.ts` asks one question for a vague question: a few plain rules, short questions with no topic, and a check on the judge's split between two passages. The visitor's answer is joined to the question and looked up.
- **Wording and next step.** Answers read "IRS Publication 17, page 110, says: ..." with one follow-up. Questions about doing something offer the site's matching form (new client, payment plan, notice, prior-year, change of address, or a consultation) and a yes opens it.
- **Repeats and narrow guides.** A passage already quoted is swapped for another that answers, or the reply says it is the same one. Publications for narrow audiences (US possessions, citizens abroad, and similar) go to the judge only when nothing general answers.
- **Short answers.** "Yeah", "no thanks" and "is it gonna take long?" are answered by code from the last topic. The desk prompt no longer allows offers it was not told it can make.
- **Tests.** Replay of call 7d689fcd (`eval/tests/conversation.test.ts`), the rule and judge tests (`copilot-engine/tests`), the judge trace through the real handler (`demo/live-site/tests/judge-trace.test.ts`).
