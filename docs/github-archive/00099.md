# Issue #99: Fix the tax demo copilot's conversation quality (vague questions, filler, judge threshold, quoting, next step)

- State: open
- Opened by Pukujan on 2026-11-01
- Original: https://github.com/Pukujan-lab/voice-agent-research/issues/99

The owner tested the Brindlewick Tax demo (https://copilot.design-bakery.com/tax) by voice on 2026-11-01 and said it is "still too dumb". The project's main goal is that a voice call does the job for the visitor so nobody has to explore the site; in this call the copilot answered the wrong question, improvised, repeated itself and never offered to start anything on the site.

Leaf: this issue. Parent: none. Related: #12 (vague questions are not asked back), #16 (risk-scored confirmation), #88 (the live-traces-to-tests loop, another session; its replay cases are used, its files are not edited). Dependencies: none.

## Symptoms (observed in the Phoenix traces of call 7d689fcd, 17:10–17:12 UTC, chatbots workspace)

1. **A vague question got a wrong answer.** "How do I find my taxes?" was answered with a Publication 970 line about viewing the amount you owe by tax year. It should have asked what they mean (their return, what they owe, a form).
2. **Filler got invented offers.** "Yeah." and "Is it gonna take long?" got "once we verify who you are..." — an offer the copilot had no reason to make.
3. **A correct answer was thrown away by our own threshold.** For "Can I file my tax return from here?" the search returned "Yes, you can file an original Form 1040 series tax return electronically using any filing status." as passage 0 and Jev (typesafe/jev-1.14) gave it 0.70 against 0.26 for "none". `JevJudge.pick` only accepts a pick when `none <= 0.25`, so the call said "I don't know". The judge span recorded an empty id, and `lastError` is never traced, so a failed Jev call looks the same as "none".
4. **Robotic quoting and a repeated passage.** Replies read "I've opened IRS Publication 570, page 31. The highlighted part says: ..." and the same Publication 570 quote came back for two different questions. Publication 570 (US possessions) is a poor pick for a general filing question, and the Publication 17 line "Fill in the lines that apply to you and attach Schedules 2 and 3" does not tell anyone how to fill a form.
5. **No next step toward doing it.** The visitor asked to be guided through filing from the site. The copilot never offered the site's forms, never asked a follow-up and never summed up.

## Proposal *(proposal: none of this exists yet)*

- (a) Code asks one clarifying question at a time for a vague question, on top of the model's decision to look something up.
- (b) The judge accepts a pick whose own probability clearly beats "none", keeps the guard against off-topic passages, records Jev's probabilities and any error on the judge span, and is pinned by a test from this call.
- (c) Answers read naturally, quote the passage once, add one follow-up offer, and offer to start the matching form on the site when the visitor wants to do the thing.
- (d) The same passage is not repeated for a different question without saying so.
- (e) "Yeah" and "is it gonna take long?" refer to the last topic or ask what is meant; no invented verification flows.
- Automated tests only (eval/ and demo/live-site/tests/) that replay these turns. No human spot checks, no model comparisons (decisions 0005, 0009).

## Acceptance

- The replayed 2026-11-01 turns give: a clarifying question for #1; a grounded "yes, you can file electronically" answer for #3; no invented flows for #2; no repeated passage for #4; a form offer for #5.
- `eval/` held-out answer numbers do not get worse.
- Deployed by moving `production`, and a replay on the live site shows the new behaviour in Phoenix.
