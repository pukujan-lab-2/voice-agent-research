# Issue #65: Copilot call widget looks bad and its view of the agent's page actions is too small to follow

- State: closed (2026-11-01)
- Opened by Pukujan on 2026-11-01
- Original: https://github.com/Pukujan-lab/voice-agent-research/issues/65
- Labels: status:resolved

### Summary

The copilot's on-page call widget looks bad, and its view of what the agent is doing on the page (filling fields, the call panel) is too small to follow. The owner wants it redesigned to the same obvious.ai-level standard as the demo sites, and big enough to watch.

### Identity and Lineage

Leaf: this issue. Parent: none. Depends on: #55's PR #61 (it edits `widget/sdk.ts`). Related: the demo site redesign issue filed alongside this one.

### Acceptance Criteria

- The call widget, chat panel and the agent's on-page activity (field highlights, what it is filling, read-backs, confirmations) are redesigned and readable at a glance on desktop and phone.
- It keeps working on every demo site and passes the scene tests and voice tests.
- It matches the new demo design system.

### Boundaries and Next Action

In scope: `demo/live-site/widget/` UI and styles. Out of scope: the conversation logic, speech stacks (#55) and logging (#17). Next action: wait for PR #61 or rebase on it, then redesign.

### Symptoms

1. **The widget looks bad.** The owner called the call agent's page-driving view "horrible".
2. **Too small to follow.** The owner said it was "too small to check".

### Hypotheses (unverified)

- **H1. A larger, clearer activity view lets the caller check each fill.** Status: unverified. Would confirm: the owner can follow each fill on screen. Would refute: still hard to follow. Experiment: none beyond the owner's review.

### Counter-signal

None.

### Honest Caveat

The widget sits on third-party pages in real use, so it must stay compact when idle.

### Problems vs Gaps

Problem: the caller can't easily see what the copilot is doing. Gap: no shared visual system between widget and sites.
