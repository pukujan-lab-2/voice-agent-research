# Issue #108: Ingest a business's own site and documents into its own Octo prefix

- State: open
- Opened by Pukujan on 2026-11-01
- Original: https://github.com/Pukujan-lab/voice-agent-research/issues/108

### Summary

A new business has no way to put its own site into Octo. The IRS library was loaded by one-off scripts that fetch, split and upload in their own way. This builds one pipeline for any business: a sitemap or start URL plus HTML, PDF and Markdown documents in, clean passages with url, title, section, page type, language and last-modified out, into that business's own Octo prefix, with an incremental re-crawl, dedupe and a count of what was indexed.

### Identity and Lineage

Leaf: this issue. Parent: #107. Depends on: none. Related: decision 0004 (Octo is the one store).

### Acceptance Criteria

- A fixture site (HTML, PDF, Markdown) loads into a business prefix with metadata on every passage and a printed count.
- A second run with nothing changed writes nothing; a changed page replaces only its passages; a deleted page's passages are removed; duplicate pages and passages are indexed once (tests).
- Two businesses never share a prefix or read each other's rows (tests).
- The choice of crawler and PDF reader is recorded on the parent issue.

### Boundaries and Next Action

In scope: new `ingest/` package, tests, CI step, docs. Out of scope: rewriting the IRS fetchers' site-specific parsing; the 880k load (#18). Next action: pick the OSS parts and write the pipeline.
