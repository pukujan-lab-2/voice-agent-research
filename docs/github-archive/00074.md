# Issue #74: Swappable decision models per task: Jev, LFM3, GLiNER2.5

- State: open
- Opened by Pukujan on 2026-11-01
- Original: https://github.com/Pukujan-lab/voice-agent-research/issues/74

### Summary

Jev 1.14 makes the copilot's yes/no and passage-pick decisions (decision 0009), and it is hard-wired. The owner thinks LFM3 could take that job, and is also looking at Fastino's GLiNER2.5 (including GLiNER2.5-Decide). Like voice stacks (decision 0012), the decision model should be an option the owner or a business picks per task, so Jev, LFM3 and GLiNER2.5 can be swapped in and used side by side.

### Identity and Lineage

Leaf: this issue. Parent: none. Depends on: none. Related: decision 0009 (LFM3 writes replies, Jev decides), decision 0006 (model routing), decision 0012 (swappable voice stacks), #71 (tool-calling test), #73 (multimodal options).

### Acceptance Criteria

- A decision-model option for each decision job (yes/no, passage pick, per-turn form decision), with a business-allowed list and a default, picked the same way as voice stacks.
- Options for Jev 1.14, LFM3 and GLiNER2.5 (the Decide classifier and/or the extractor, per their model cards), each callable through one interface.
- The existing automated evals (grounding, verification, read-backs) run on whichever option is picked, with results recorded per option.
- A decision record replaces the fixed routing in 0006/0009 with the option registry.

### Boundaries and Next Action

In scope: `copilot-engine/` decision calls, the backend routing in `demo/live-site/api.ts`, a GLiNER2.5 adapter, the decision record. Out of scope: reply-writing (LFM3 stays, decision 0009), voice stacks, multimodal (#73). Next action: read Fastino's official GLiNER2.5 and GLiNER2.5-Decide model cards for exact checkpoint names, sizes, license and labels, and post them here; the owner approves any download.

### Symptoms

1. **Jev is the only decision model.** Decisions 0006 and 0009 route yes/no and passage picks to Jev 1.14 with no option to change it.
2. **No GLiNER path.** Nothing in the repo mentions GLiNER.

### Hypotheses (unverified)

- **H1. LFM3 can make Jev's decisions.** Status: unverified. Support: the LFM3-1.7B model card says it is built for tool calling and structured output and lists BFCL V4 71.39. Would refute: the existing evals fail on it.
- **H2. GLiNER2.5-Decide fits yes/no and pick decisions.** Status: unverified. Source: secondary aggregators describe it as a 340M English, Apache-2.0 classifier for operational decisions, released 2026-09-24. Would refute: its card says it does not support custom labels.

### Counter-signal

GLiNER2.5-Decide is described as English only, so it may not cover the multilingual stack.

### Honest Caveat

GLiNER2.5 facts come from secondary sites, not Fastino's own cards. A separate community "GLiNER v2.5" family exists; check model IDs.

### Problems vs Gaps

Gap: decision models are fixed, against the owner's rule that every model is swappable per task.
