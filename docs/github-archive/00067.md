The demo pages still looked plain next to the reference the owner chose, and the five fictional businesses read as generic templates beside the tax firm.

This finishes the redesign: one design system across the product page and the five demo sites.

## What changes

- **One system, in one place.** Warm paper, ink and clay, one card language, pill buttons, one focus ring, defined in `demo/live-site/app/src/styles/design.css`.
- **The businesses get a serif.** Their headings use the same serif the tax firm already uses, so a clinic or a pension fund reads as a business rather than a template. The product page keeps its grotesque, because it is selling software.
- **One hero everywhere.** Copy on the left, one original illustration on the right (`SiteHeroArt`), drawn in that site's own accent colour.
- **Defects the review found, fixed.** The hero paragraph that sat centred under a left-aligned heading (all five sites); the product page heading that broke after "fills in"; a navigation bar that stopped at 78rem and left white gutters at the top; and the header's "Leave" link that sat under the guest label.
