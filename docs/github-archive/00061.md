Visitors need to choose a voice and language before a call, and switch language when their needs change. This slice adds a local voice lab with four combinations of Parakeet or Whisper and Pocket TTS or Kokoro. It lists other models as unavailable until their adapters exist.

A caller can select a compatible language before or during a call. Switching from the English-only stack to Spanish reconnects the voice session while retaining the conversation. The same form decision and confirmation rules continue to apply.

Recorded-audio tests passed 11 of 11 checks, covering all four combinations, English, Spanish, French, and an English-to-Spanish switch. The repository checks, React build and copilot contract pass. The call tests demonstrate connectivity and language handling; they are not a comparison of model quality.
