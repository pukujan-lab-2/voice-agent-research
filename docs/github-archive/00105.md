# PR #105: Show the model a fixed-size window of a big form instead of every field

- State: open
- Opened by Pukujan on 2026-11-01
- Original: https://github.com/Pukujan-lab/voice-agent-research/pull/105
- Branch `task/WCP-0040-prompt-budget` into `main`, +376 −22 in 7 files

A form with 300 fields put about 100,000 characters in front of the small model on every turn, so a business with a big form could overflow its context.

## What changes

- A form of up to 24 fields is shown to the model exactly as before.
- A bigger form shows the model a window of at most 14 fields: the open one, a read-back that is waiting, where to return to, the next 4 empty ones, the last 3 filled and up to 3 the caller just named, plus a count of the rest. Code keeps every answer. The tool schema stops listing every field name, and a choice with more than 12 options shows 12 and a count.
- A long call no longer grows without a bound in memory: the call log keeps its first line and the latest 40 entries, and the history keeps 12 user turns.
- `docs/scaling-limits.md` records these limits.

## Before and after

| Largest request while filling a whole form (characters) | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| 10 fields | 9,572 | 9,572 |
| 100 fields | 37,133 | 9,018 |
| 300 fields | 101,072 | 9,097 |

## How to verify

3. The new tests fill a 300-field form over 600 turns and check every value is in its own field, run a 100-turn call, and check that a field the caller names is shown to the model, read back and filled in its own place while the open field is untouched.

## What stays unchanged

The prompt, tools and behaviour for forms of 24 fields or fewer; the confirmation rules; the server and widget. A field the caller never names and that isn't in the window can't be filled by the model that turn (code still asks for it in order).
