Help and FAQ

Opening hours

Monday to Friday 8:00 to 18:00, Saturday 9:00 to 13:00.

How do I cancel or move a visit?

Use the link in your confirmation text, or call the front desk at least 24 hours before.

What should I bring?

Your child's health booklet and a list of any medicines they take.

My child has a high fever right now

Book a sick visit for today. If your child is very drowsy or struggling to breathe, call emergency services.
