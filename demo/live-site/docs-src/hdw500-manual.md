# About this manual

This manual covers the Kettlewisp HDW-500 built-in dishwasher. Kettlewisp Appliances is a fictional company made up for a software demo. Read the safety notes before you use the dishwasher for the first time.

# Safety notes

Always turn the dishwasher off at the wall switch before you clean the filter or reach into the tub. Do not open the door during a program unless the display shows PAUSE. Keep detergent away from children. Hot water can stay in the tub for up to 10 minutes after a program stops.

# Where to find the serial number

The serial number is printed on a silver sticker on the right edge of the inner door. You see it when the door is open. It has 12 characters, letters and digits, for example 1334-592C-LSD3. Quote it whenever you ask for service.

# Installation notes

The drain hose must not be higher than 1000 mm above the floor. Do not kink or squash the drain hose behind the dishwasher. A kinked hose is the most common reason for slow draining after installation.

# Detergent and rinse aid

Use only detergent and rinse aid made for dishwashers. Washing-up liquid makes too much foam and can make water leak from the door. Fill the rinse aid compartment when the rinse aid light comes on, about once a month.

# Loading

Put cups and glasses in the top rack and plates, pots and pans in the bottom rack. Large items must not block the spray arms. Scrape food off plates before loading; you do not need to rinse them.

<!-- page -->
# Cleaning the filter

Clean the filter once a month, or every two weeks if you load dishes with a lot of food left on them. The filter sits in the middle of the tub floor, under the bottom rack. It has two parts: a coarse cup and a fine mesh.

Turn the filter a quarter turn to the left and lift it straight up. Rinse both parts under warm running water and scrub gently with a soft brush. Do not use a wire brush or steel wool; they damage the fine mesh. To put it back, line up the arrow on the cup with the arrow on the floor and turn it right until it clicks.

# E24: water is not draining

Standing water at the bottom of the tub after a program, together with E24 on the display, almost always means the filter is blocked. Follow these steps:

1. Press and hold Start for 3 seconds to cancel the program. Wait 10 minutes for the water to cool.
2. Turn the dishwasher off at the wall switch.
3. Pull out the bottom rack and lay a towel in front of the dishwasher.
4. Turn the filter a quarter turn to the left, lift it out and rinse it under warm water.
5. Check behind the dishwasher that the drain hose is not kinked or squashed.
6. Put the filter back until it clicks, turn the power on and run the Rinse program (12 min).

If E24 comes back after the Rinse program, do not run the dishwasher again. Request a service visit.

# Warranty and service

The HDW-500 has a 2-year parts warranty and a 1-year labour warranty from the date of purchase. Damage from using a wire brush on the filter, or from using washing-up liquid, is not covered. A service visit inside the warranty is free if the fault is covered.

To request a service visit, use the service request form on the Kettlewisp support page. Have the serial number and the error code ready. A technician usually visits within 3 working days.
