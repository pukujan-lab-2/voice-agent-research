# What form R-17 is for

Form R-17 tells Saltmere Pension Fund who should receive your pension benefits if you die. Saltmere Pension Fund is a fictional fund made up for a software demo. A new R-17 replaces every earlier R-17 you have sent us.

# When to send a new R-17

Send a new form after a marriage, a divorce, the birth or adoption of a child, or the death of a beneficiary. If you never send an R-17, benefits are paid to your estate.

# Who can be a beneficiary

Any person, your estate, or a trust can be a beneficiary. A beneficiary does not need to be a member of the fund.

<!-- page -->
# Section A: your details

Enter your member number exactly as it appears on your annual statement. It starts with SPF and has 6 digits, for example SPF-204118.

# Section B: your beneficiaries

A primary beneficiary receives benefits first. A contingent beneficiary receives benefits only if no primary beneficiary is alive. The shares of all primary beneficiaries must add up to 100 percent. The shares of all contingent beneficiaries must also add up to 100 percent.

You can name up to 4 primary and 4 contingent beneficiaries on one form.

# Payee codes

The payee code is a single letter that says how the beneficiary is related to you. It does not change how much the beneficiary receives.

If you name your estate or a trust, leave the beneficiary name fields empty and attach the trust deed for code T.

# Spousal consent

If you are married and you name someone other than your spouse as primary beneficiary for more than 50 percent, your spouse must sign Section D in front of a witness.

# Signing and witness

Sign Section C yourself, in front of one witness. The witness must be 18 or older and must not be a beneficiary on the form.
