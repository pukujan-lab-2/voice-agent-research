Safe by design

Fills only what you confirm

It asks one question at a time, reads back emails, phone and serial numbers, and types an answer in only after you confirm.

Never presses Submit

When the form is done, it hands it back. You check the page and press the button yourself.

Answers from your documents

Questions are answered by quoting your own manual, with the page number. If the manual doesn't say, it says "I don't know".

Your data stays in house

Customer details and documents stay in your own systems. Decisions are made in code, not by a model guessing.

How it works

  1. Add one script tag to your site. The copilot reads the form that's already on the page.
  2. A visitor taps Start voice call (or types). The copilot asks for each field in plain words.
  3. It fills each answer in as it's confirmed, explains terms from your documents, and hands the form back for the visitor to submit.
