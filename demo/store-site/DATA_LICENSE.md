# Brambleway demo data

Brambleway is a fictional store. Everything it shows was made for this demo. None of it is copied from a real
store, and none of it describes real products, people or companies.

| Data | Where it comes from |
| --- | --- |
| Catalogue: products, brands, variants, prices, stock, deals, specs and descriptions | Generated by `tools/gen_catalog.py` from the hand-written taxonomy in `tools/taxonomy.py` (fixed seed `20261031`, so a re-run gives the same ids and text). Brand names are invented word pairs. |
| Reviews and questions and answers | Generated by the same script from short templates. They are labelled as fictional demo reviews wherever they appear. |
| Product images | Simple SVG illustrations drawn by `tools/gen_images.py` (shapes and the product's category), stored in Octo files. They are not photos. |
| Help centre articles | Written for this demo in `tools/help.py`. The policies (30-day returns, shipping prices and so on) are Brambleway's own and apply to nothing real. |

Everything is stored in Octo (workspace `octo_db_chatbots`, tables `shop_*`, image files named `shop-img-*`, mapped in `shop_images`). The site has
no local copy and no fallback.

The demo takes no real payments and ships nothing. Orders placed on the site are demo records that only move along a
pretend tracking clock.
